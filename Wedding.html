The four Lavaan

A Sacred Journey

During the Anand Karaj, the couple circles Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji four times, each round a step on a shared spiritual path.

1

First Lav

Commitment to a righteous life, guided by the Guru’s word.

2

Second Lav

Letting go of ego, walking forward with humility and devotion.

3

Third Lav

Detachment from worldly worries, finding joy in the sangat.

4

Fourth Lav

Union in peace and bliss: two souls becoming one light.
